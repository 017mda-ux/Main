Alphabet DCF, step by step

Your case, in $ billions. A DCF asks one question: what are all of Alphabet's future cash flows worth in today's money? Tap any bar for its number.

1

Sales grow, mostly from Cloud

Revenue goes from $492B in 2026 to $969B in 2031. Search grows slowly (+5%, then +2.5%). Cloud more than quadruples.

2

Profit grows, but slower than sales

Operating profit rises from $186B to $289B. Search profit stays flat at about $130B because its margin falls from 52% to 40%. Cloud supplies almost all of the growth. The orange bars below zero are company-wide costs (AI research, Other Bets).

3

Cash in vs cash spent on data centers

Cash the business makes = profit after tax + depreciation added back. Capex is spent on top. The gap is free cash flow, the cash left for owners. It stays negative until 2030.

4

Future dollars are worth less today

You need an 8.15% return a year (your cost of equity). So $1 received in 2031 is worth only 68¢ today. Each year's cash flow is shrunk by that factor.

Add the five years up and they're worth −$32B today. The heavy spending eats all of the near-term cash.

7

Move k and g yourself

Terminal value = 2031 cash flow × (1 + g) ÷ (k − g). k is your required return, g is growth forever. Drag either one and watch the value change.
