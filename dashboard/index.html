What actually moved

Ranked by standard deviations against each instrument’s own trailing 60-day volatility, not by headline percentage — a 0.4% move in the 10-year is a bigger event than a 1.5% move in the Nasdaq.

Signal crosses

Ratios that carry economic information the constituent legs do not.

Indices against their own history

Percentile of each index against its own price history, and distance from the 200-day mean. Each row states the window it used.

These are price-history percentiles, not forward multiples. No free, keyless, browser-accessible source publishes forward P/E — so this page does not pretend to. For forward multiples against long-run averages, the primary sources are linked under Valuation in the source rail.

The desk

Deep links to the page that holds the data — never a homepage, never secondary commentary.

Market series are fetched in your browser from Stooq on each load, and fall back to the snapshot committed by the refresh job. Headlines and filings come only from the issuing institution. Nothing on this page is interpolated, estimated or modelled — if a number could not be sourced it reads “—”.
